Engineered Features

A Superior Anime Client, Built Without Compromise.

Every component in Zenith Anime is purpose-built for speed, privacy, and immersion.

ExoPlayer Video Suite

Hardware-accelerated rendering with full audio track selection (including verified English dub switching), multi-language subtitle parsing, 0.25x–3.0x speed, and hold-to-speed gestures.

Local-First Architecture

All watchlist items, watch progress, and custom preferences are stored locally in a Room database with Moshi JSON serialization. No cloud account required, zero tracking, and complete offline backup & restore.

Remastered Continue Watching

Widescreen 16:9 thumbnails with duration remaining badges, episode progress percentages, a dedicated Info button to navigate directly to anime details, and one-tap resume.

AniList & MyAnimeList Reviews

Richly formatted community reviews with inline illustrations, interactive spoiler tags, and score breakdowns directly on anime details pages.

Watchlist Organization

Categorize collections into Watching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, and Plan to Watch. Includes smart status filtering, sorting, and seamless imported anime management.

Profile Customization

Full support for custom avatar URLs, SVG vectors, and animated GIFs with defensive image validation and sandboxed preview.

Interactive Engine

Live Video Player Playground

Test layout modifications in real time. Zenith Anime lets users configure control positions, visibility, seekbars, and lock placement without writing a single line of code.

Zenith Anime does not host, store, upload, or distribute any media files on any server. Comprehensive DMCA & Legal Policy

1. Non-Hosting Statement: Zenith Anime is an open-source software application that functions strictly as an indexing client. It does not own, produce, host, store, or transmit any video streams, audiovisual media files, or copyright-protected content on any server.


2. Third-Party Indexing: The links, media streams, and metadata rendered within the client are retrieved dynamically from publicly available, unauthenticated third-party providers on the public web. Availability and stream health remain under the sole discretion of those independent external sources.


3. Takedown & Infringement Notices: As Zenith Anime maintains no media servers or databases of hosted media, removing content from Zenith Anime cannot delete the content from the internet. Copyright holders seeking removal must direct copyright infringement notices to the specific third-party service hosting the file.
